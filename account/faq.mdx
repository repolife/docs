---
title: "FAQ"
description: "Answers to common questions about Seed Scroll."
---

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I need an internet connection to read the Bible?">
    No. Supported Bible translations are available locally after install or download. You can read any downloaded translation offline. Features that require an internet connection include AI-powered tools, social content, sync, first-time translation downloads, and first-time interlinear downloads.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What Bible translations does Seed Scroll support?">
    Seed Scroll supports KJV+, WEB, RV1909, and BSB. KJV+, RV1909, and BSB include native Strong's tagging. WEB uses KJV-backed verse-level Strong's study because WEB does not include native Strong's data. See [Bible Translations](/features/bible-translations) for details.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Does Seed Scroll support Spanish?">
    Yes. Seed Scroll supports RV1909 Spanish Bible reading, Spanish app language settings, Spanish book names in supported flows, and Spanish AI output where supported. See [Spanish Support](/getting-started/spanish-support).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I import a reading plan from a PDF or CSV?">
    Yes. You can import a filled CSV template, PDF, Word document, plain text, markdown, or pasted schedule text. CSV is the most reliable option. See [Reading Plans](/features/reading-plans#import-a-reading-plan).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is the interlinear database downloaded separately?">
    Yes. The interlinear database (~46 MB) is downloaded the first time you open the interlinear view. After that, it's stored locally and available offline. Make sure you have a Wi-Fi connection for the initial download.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my subscription?">
    Subscriptions are managed through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). To cancel, open your device's subscription settings and cancel the Seed Scroll subscription from there. Your premium access remains active until the end of the current billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Seed Scroll on multiple devices?">
    Yes. Sign in with the same account on any device and your study notes, reading position, and settings will sync automatically.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What happens to my notes if I delete the app?">
    If you're signed in, your notes are synced to your account and can be recovered by reinstalling and signing in. If you're using guest mode, notes are stored only on your device — deleting the app permanently removes them.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why does transcription take a while?">
    Audio transcription runs entirely on your device using the Whisper model — nothing is uploaded to a server. Processing time depends on the recording length and your device's performance. A 30-minute recording typically takes 2–5 minutes to transcribe.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I use Seed Scroll in a browser?">
    Yes. The web version runs in any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge). It uses the same reading and study features where browser APIs support them, and Bible data can be cached for offline reading after the initial load.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I report a bug or give feedback?">
    Shake your device (or look for the feedback option in the app menu) to open the in-app feedback form. You can also use the feedback button in your profile settings.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Is Strong's Concordance available for free?">
    Yes. Tapping any word to see its Strong's number, definition, transliteration, and pronunciation is a free feature available to all users including guests.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
