Your profile is your public presence in the Seed Scroll community. It shows your published study notes, reading plans, and follower counts.

## Edit your profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile">
    Tap **Profile** in the bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the pencil icon">
    Tap the pencil icon in the header to open the editor.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your details">
    You can change your:
    - Display name
    - Profile photo
    - Bio
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Tap **Save** to apply your changes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your public content

Your profile page shows:
- Published study notes
- Published reading plans
- Follower and following counts

Only what you have published appears here. Studies and plans you have kept private are not shown, and neither is anything you have saved or bookmarked.

Other Seed Scroll users can open your profile from anywhere your name appears — the feed, a study, or Discover. The community features that lead there need a free account; see [Guest mode](/getting-started/guest-mode) for what reading without one gives you.

## Saved content

Tap **Saved** on your profile to open your saved studies, Bible verses, playlists, collections, and verse images without first opening the Studies tab.

What you have saved is yours — it does not appear on your profile and nobody can browse it.

One thing to know: when you bookmark someone else's **public** study, its author is notified that it was saved. What you saved is not shown to anyone else, and saving your own work notifies no one.

## Creator Dashboard

Tap the chart icon in the header to see how your public work is doing: your follower count, total likes across your studies, total enrollments across your reading plans, then each study ordered by likes and each plan ordered by enrollments.

The dashboard is on your own profile only — you cannot see anyone else's, and there is no view count.

## Sign out

To sign out, tap **Profile** → **Settings** (gear icon) → **Sign Out**. Your notes and reading position are saved to your account and will be restored when you sign back in.
