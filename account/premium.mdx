Seed Scroll offers a premium subscription with monthly and annual billing options. Premium unlocks AI-powered tools and full community features.

## What's included in premium

- **AI Bible search** — ask questions in plain English to find relevant verses instantly
- **AI study generation** — generate a structured study from text, documents, images, or audio
- **AI reading plans** — create custom multi-day reading schedules on any topic
- **Hebrew letter analysis** — pictographic meanings for every letter in a Hebrew word
- **Greek semantic synthesis** — AI-generated thematic analysis of Greek words
- **Social feed access** — browse community notes and follow other Bible students
- **Publish notes and plans** — share your content publicly on your profile

## Subscribe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Paywall screen">
    Tap **Profile** → **Go Premium**, or tap the upgrade prompt that appears when you try to use a premium feature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a plan">
    Select **Monthly** or **Annual**. The annual plan offers a significant discount compared to monthly billing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm purchase">
    Complete the purchase through the App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). On web, payment is processed through the same subscription.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Restore a purchase

If you subscribed previously and need to restore your access on a new device:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to your account">
    Make sure you're signed in with the same account used when you subscribed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Restore Purchases">
    On the Paywall screen, tap **Restore Purchases**. Your subscription status is fetched and restored automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Subscriptions are managed through the App Store or Google Play. To cancel, go to your subscription settings in the App Store or Google Play — not within the Seed Scroll app.
</Note>

## Free features

The following features are always free, with or without a subscription:

- Full Bible reader (KJV+ with 31,102 verses)
- Strong's Concordance definitions
- Interlinear view (Hebrew & Greek)
- Cross-references
- Personal study notes (local and synced)
- Manual reading plan creation
- Dark/light theme
