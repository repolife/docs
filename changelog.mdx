## September 24, 2026 (web)

### Added

- Added **Atlas** to the web app: a 3D map of every place the Bible names, drawn over real terrain. Move through the eras from the Patriarchs to Acts, switch place labels between English and the Cepher's Hebrew names, open a place to read the verses that name it, and turn 3D reconstructions of Babylon, the Temple and the Tabernacle in a full-screen viewer. See [Atlas](/features/atlas).
- Added narrated **journeys** in Atlas for premium members: Abraham's journey, the Exodus, Jesus' last week and Paul's first journey play stop by stop, with a spoken narrator, the verses to read at each stop, and the distance of every leg. Any journey can become a generated study or a reading plan, one stop a day.
- Added the **Atlas guide** for premium members. Ask where something happened and the map flies there with the verses that tell it; ask for a road and the guide composes a journey on the spot.
- Added **AI Connections**: connect Claude or ChatGPT to your own bookmarks, study notes and reading plans, and see or disconnect your connected assistants under **Profile** → **Reader Settings** → **AI Connections** in the web app. See [AI Connections](/account/ai-connections).
