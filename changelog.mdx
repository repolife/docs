## August 5–10, 2026

### Added

- Added **AI generation alerts**. Start an AI study or reading plan, then leave the app — a notification tells you when it is ready, or if it could not be finished. Opening the app from the notification takes you to the finished study or plan, and if you are still in the app your phone gives a short buzz instead.

### Fixed

- Fixed device notifications for social activity. New followers, likes, copies, saves, enrollments, plan completions, and studies published by creators you follow now reach your phone. Daily reading reminders were unaffected.
- Fixed the Bible reader walkthrough highlighting the wrong part of the screen. The opening step now reliably frames Genesis 1:1, and the header steps point at the button they describe.
- Fixed the walkthrough being skipped for the rest of a session when the reader was opened on a specific verse or inside a reading plan.

## July 28–August 4, 2026

### Added

- Added configurable **Whole-Bible Reading Plans** from the Plans tab. Choose a one-, two-, or three-year schedule, read every day or on weekdays, select a start date, and follow either traditional Bible order or Seed Scroll's approximate chronological order.
- Added guided **AI Reading Plan** creation for premium members. Describe your goal, choose a preset or custom length up to 365 days, and select an every-day or weekdays-only schedule.
- Added a complete review step for generated plans. You can inspect every scheduled day, Scripture reference, and daily study before saving, choose a brief reflection or deeper mini-study format, and make the plan private or public during creation.
- Added full reading-plan PDF exports. Share or save a print-friendly document containing the plan description, schedule, dates, passages, and daily content on iOS, Android, or web.
- Added support for one-time purchases of licensed Bible translations, beginning with **Cepher - The Way, the Truth, and the Life**. The purchase flow supports restoring ownership, securely downloading the translation, and reading it offline after installation.
- Added mapped Strong's word-study links for supported Cepher WTL words, including mapped divine-name forms.

### Improved

- Improved reading-plan creation so the plan, visibility, schedule, and all generated days are saved together. Plan creators are also enrolled automatically so reminders and progress controls work immediately.
- Improved the Plans screen by keeping existing plans visible while refreshed data loads in the background.
- Improved reading-plan reminder recovery and permission guidance, including a direct **Open Settings** action when notifications are disabled.
- Improved long AI plans so their titles and descriptions describe the full requested duration instead of only the first generation batch.
- Improved the translation picker with the full Cepher WTL name, localized one-time pricing, ownership and download status, and controlled publisher QA access before public activation.
- Improved licensed translation downloads with authenticated delivery, file integrity checks, and entitlement synchronization across purchases and restores.
- Improved the Strong's **Add to Note** flow with a themed chooser for creating a note or selecting an existing one while preserving the English word, original-language lemma, and Strong's number.
- Improved Spanish AI studies so translated output preserves the expected localized divine names.
- Updated the Privacy Policy to explain account information, submitted media, AI prompts, religious content, usage data, purchases, diagnostics, service providers, and deletion choices more clearly.

### Fixed

- Fixed reading-plan reminders that could appear disabled even though a notification remained scheduled, and ensured deleting a plan also cleans up its scheduled reminder.
- Fixed Chapter Guide questions carrying into a different chapter or translation after navigation.
