## August 20–24, 2026

### Improved

- Improved the purchase screen for licensed translations so it names each phase of the work after the download finishes. Checking and installing used to run under **Downloading… 100%** for around twenty seconds, which read as a hang. The label now follows the work, and the bar stops reporting a percentage once there are no bytes left to count.
- Improved the emails Seed Scroll sends about your account. Signup confirmation, password reset, and email change now share one design — the brand palette, a single clear action, and the link repeated as readable text underneath so a blocked button is never a dead end. All three are legible in dark mode. An email-change message names the address it is changing to, because it arrives at the old inbox and the new one alike.
- Improved the Hebrew letter card in the Strong's definition modal. It now draws the letter's Proto-Sinaitic glyph in the same treatment the word study panel uses, rather than a system emoji that every platform redraws its own way.

### Fixed

- Fixed a cold start that could sit on the launch screen and, on iOS, be closed by the system before the app opened. Everything the reader needed arrived over the network first — around 110MB, in series — and no verse rendered until all of it landed. Bible text now comes with the app, so a first launch reaches a readable chapter without touching the network at all. Strong's, cross-reference, and interlinear data install afterwards, one at a time, and a screen whose data has not arrived yet shows its progress instead of an error.
- Fixed the verse study panel staying empty for a long time on a new install. Strong's data is the smallest of the three background installs, but it was waiting for the other two to finish first. The panel now waits only on what it needs, moves that to the front of the queue, and shows progress while it arrives — what you have open decides what installs next.
- Fixed a shared link being replaced by an older notification when it opened the app from cold. Whichever of the two arrived first used to decide where you landed.
- Fixed a translation you had just bought going missing from the translation picker for up to twenty-five seconds after it finished installing. The picker no longer waits on the store before listing what is already on your device.
- Fixed shared links naming **Cepher WTL** as only "WTL". A shared link is opened by someone who did not choose the translation, so licensed editions are now written out in full. Public translations keep the short labels readers recognise.
- Fixed gold running past the close of a quotation and into the narration after it in **WEB**. Matthew 8:26 gave the narrator's "Then" to Yeshua, and 9:22 and 9:24 took words that follow the quotation.
- Fixed the signup alert on iOS wrapping so that the sentence's full stop was stranded at the start of the next line, away from the address it followed.

## August 19–20, 2026

### Improved

- Improved the daily notes in AI-generated reading plans. A generated note used to arrive as one unbroken paragraph with its labels — Context, Key insight, Application, Reflect — buried in the sentences. Each is now a heading of its own, and **Reflect** sits apart at the foot of the note, where it hands off to your own reflection directly beneath it. Plans generated before this change read the new way too, and nothing you had saved was rewritten.
- Improved link previews for shared Seed Scroll links. A preview card now credits the account that shared it and describes its image for anyone reading with a screen reader.

### Fixed

- Fixed account deletion requests that could be closed without the account being deleted. A request that cannot be matched is now kept for another attempt.

## August 16–19, 2026

### Added

- Added **shareable links for verse images**. **Share** still hands over an image file that never leaves your phone. **Share link** publishes a copy and gives you an address anyone can open, whether or not they have Seed Scroll. You can unshare a link afterwards, which deletes the published copy.
- Added a confirmation when you save a verse image. It names where the image went — **Studies → Verse images** — and offers to open it, instead of closing the editor without a word.

### Improved

- Improved the reader header so that book, chapter, and verse each open the step they name. The chapter number opens that book's chapter grid with your current chapter highlighted, the verse number opens the verses of the chapter you are in, and the book name opens the list on your own testament with your book already scrolled into view. All three used to open the same list at Genesis.
- Improved playlist visibility. The overview now shows it as a badge you can read but not change, and the pencil opens **Edit Playlist**, where studies and reading plans already keep it. Publishing or unpublishing is no longer one stray tap on the screen you open to listen. Playlist and reading plan badges are both read out in full by a screen reader.

### Fixed

- Fixed publishing an audio playlist failing with an error, and the same fault silently abandoning a note copy.
- Fixed **WEB** giving Yeshua words from the narration that introduces his speech. Matthew 9:2 set "said to the paralytic," in gold, and Matthew 8:4 put "to" in his mouth.
- Fixed shared verse links losing the translation they were shared from. A link now carries it, so one shared from **RV1909** opens in RV1909 and names the passage as Juan 5:6-8 rather than John 5:6-8 over Spanish scripture. A link to a licensed translation you do not own takes you to that translation's screen with the passage, rather than quietly showing you different text. Links shared before this change carry no translation and open as they always have.

## August 14–16, 2026

### Added

- Added **blocking**. Block an account from any study, verse note, reading plan, or playlist it wrote. The confirmation states every consequence before you agree to it: you stop seeing their studies, verse notes, reading plans, and playlists and they stop seeing yours, any follow between you is removed, notifications between you are deleted, and they are not told they have been blocked. **Blocked accounts** in Reader Settings lists everyone you have blocked and the date it happened, and you can unblock at any time — which restores what the two of you can see of each other, but not the follow.

### Fixed

- Fixed nested quotation marks reading as fewer marks than scripture has. Where a quotation opens inside a quotation — Ezekiel 6:8 in **WEB** opens three — the marks were set flush against each other and merged. A hair space now separates them, so each mark reads on its own while the group stays one cluster.

## August 13–15, 2026

### Added

- Added **collections for saved studies**. Create a named collection in Saved — Marriage, Torah, Studies to revisit — give it a description if the name is not enough on its own, and file saved studies into it. A study can sit in as many collections as you like without being copied, and collections are private: the person who wrote a study never sees that you saved it or what you called the collection it went into.
- Added an **Add to collection** action to the confirmation shown when you save a study, so a study can be filed the moment you save it rather than hunted down later.
- Added an **Add studies** button inside a collection, so one you have just created can be filled without leaving it.

### Improved

- Improved the Saved screen. **All Saved Studies** now stays listed even when empty, so the place saved studies live is always where you left it. Each collection shows its description and how much is in it, and its icon reflects what it holds — a mortarboard for studies, an open book for verses.
- Improved rows inside a collection so each says what it is: your own study, a study saved from another reader, a Bible verse, or a chapter.
- Improved collection names. Blank names, names longer than the field can show, and names you already have are refused with a reason under the field instead of a failure after the fact.
- Improved deleting a collection so it only unfiles. The studies inside stay saved under All Saved Studies, and removing a study from one collection leaves it in every other.
- Improved the confirmation shown after deleting. It is centred, and it says what happened in full — **Reading plan has been deleted** rather than *Reading plan deleted*. Removing a bookmark still says **Bookmark removed**, because a bookmark is removed rather than destroyed.
- Improved **The Way, the Truth & the Life** so it is read under the names its own edition prints. The book picker shows the publisher's common name with the printed title beneath it — Tehilliym above The Psalms — across three columns rather than one long list, and the reader header leads with the printed title.

### Fixed

- Fixed a republished Spanish **RVR1909** never reaching readers who already had the older copy, and a public translation going stale when no bundled copy existed to fall back on.
- Fixed the search icon sliding off the reader header when the book name ran long.
- Fixed the chapter grid collapsing to a single row on shorter screens, which put later chapters out of reach.
- Fixed walkthrough steps highlighting empty space where a button was expected.
- Fixed collections and their descriptions staying on the device that made them. They now travel with your account, including collections you have not filed anything into yet, and deleting or renaming one on one device carries to the rest instead of coming back on the next sign-in.
