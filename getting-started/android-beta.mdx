# Help Us Bring Seed Scroll to Google Play

Seed Scroll is getting closer to launching on Google Play, and we need Android users to help us complete the final closed-testing phase.

As a beta tester, you'll receive early access to Seed Scroll and Premium features while helping us make the app smoother, more reliable, and ready for launch.

## What you'll be asked to do

To participate, you'll need to:

- Have an Android phone or tablet with access to Google Play
- Join the closed beta using the Google Account connected to your Play Store
- Install Seed Scroll through the private Google Play testing link
- Remain opted in to the beta for at least 14 consecutive days
- Use the app during the testing period
- Share honest feedback and report any problems you encounter

You do not need technical experience. We are looking for people who regularly read or study the Bible and are willing to use Seed Scroll during the beta.

## What you'll receive

- Premium access during the testing period
- Early access to Seed Scroll before its public launch
- Opportunities to test new Bible reading and study features
- A direct role in helping shape the app

## About Seed Scroll

Seed Scroll is a Bible reading and study app designed to help you go deeper into Scripture.

Features include:

- Multiple Bible translations
- Strong's and original-language study tools
- Audio Bible playback
- AI-powered studies and reading plans
- Historical and chapter context
- Notes, bookmarks, and Quick Reflections
- Cross-references and translation comparison
- Saved Verse Images
- Immersive Bible reading modes

## Join or request access

If your Google Account has already been approved, open the [Seed Scroll Android beta](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.anonymous.socialbibleapp), tap **Become a tester**, and follow the Google Play installation link.

If you have not been added yet, [request beta access by email](mailto:contact@seedscroll.app?subject=Seed%20Scroll%20Android%20Beta%20Access). Send the request from—or include—the same Google Account email connected to the Play Store on your Android device. We will use that address only to arrange beta access and communicate testing instructions.

## What happens next?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request access">
    Send us the Google Account email used by the Play Store on your Android device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Watch for confirmation">
    We will confirm when your account has been added to the closed-test group and send the private Google Play opt-in link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Opt in and install">
    Open the link while using the approved Google Account, tap **Become a tester**, and install Seed Scroll through Google Play.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test for at least 14 days">
    Remain opted in for at least 14 consecutive days, use Seed Scroll regularly, and send us honest feedback.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Requesting access does not automatically enroll you in the beta. Please watch your email for confirmation and setup instructions.

## Questions or feedback?

Email [contact@seedscroll.app](mailto:contact@seedscroll.app?subject=Seed%20Scroll%20Android%20Beta%20Help). You can also send private beta feedback through Google Play.
