## System requirements

| Platform | Minimum requirement |
| -------- | ------------------- |
| iOS / iPadOS | iOS 15 or later |
| Android  | Android 8.0 (Oreo) or later |
| Web      | Any modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge) |

## Get the app

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="iOS">
    You can install Seed Scroll for iPhone and iPad through TestFlight if you are an invited tester.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Accept the TestFlight invitation">
        Open the Seed Scroll invitation email on your iPhone or iPad and tap **View in TestFlight**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install TestFlight">
        If prompted, install Apple's free **TestFlight** app from the App Store.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install Seed Scroll">
        Return to the invitation, tap **View in TestFlight**, and then tap **Install**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open and start reading">
        Tap **Open** in TestFlight and complete the short onboarding flow. You can start reading immediately. No Seed Scroll account is required.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      You currently need a TestFlight invitation. After the public iOS release, you can use a direct App Store link from this page.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Android">
    You can install Seed Scroll for Android through Google Play closed testing.

    <Card title="Join the Seed Scroll Android beta" icon="android" href="https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.anonymous.socialbibleapp">
      Open the private Google Play opt-in page.
    </Card>

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Use your registered Google Account">
        Sign in with the same Google Account that was added to the Seed Scroll closed-test group.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Join the closed beta">
        Open the [Seed Scroll Android beta](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.anonymous.socialbibleapp) and tap **Become a tester**.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Install the app">
        Follow the Google Play link shown after you join, then tap **Install**. If Google Play granted access recently, wait a few minutes for the app to appear.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Open and start reading">
        Tap **Open** when the download finishes, or find Seed Scroll in your app drawer.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      If Google Play says the app is unavailable, confirm that the current Google Account belongs to the closed-test group and then reopen the beta link. See the [Android beta guide](/getting-started/android-beta) for requirements and help.
    </Note>
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Web">
    No download needed on desktop or mobile web.

    <Steps>
      <Step title="Navigate to the web app">
        Open your browser and go to the Seed Scroll web address. The app loads fully in the browser tab.
      </Step>
      <Step title="Start reading">
        The full Bible reader, Strong's concordance, and interlinear view are available in the browser. Sign in with email, Google, or Apple to sync your notes across devices.
      </Step>
    </Steps>

    <Note>
      On first load, the web app downloads and caches the Bible data in your browser. Subsequent visits work offline without re-downloading.
    </Note>

    <Tip>
      On mobile browsers you can add Seed Scroll to your home screen via **Share → Add to Home Screen** for a full-screen, app-like experience without going through an app store.
    </Tip>
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Offline reading

The default Bible reader is available for offline use, and additional supported translations can be downloaded for offline reading. You do not need an internet connection to:

- Read any book and chapter
- Look up Strong's numbers and definitions
- View cross-references
- Write and edit local study notes

An internet connection is required for AI features, syncing notes to your account, and accessing the social feed.

## First-launch interlinear download

The Bible reader works immediately after installation. The first time you open the **Interlinear** view, the app downloads the interlinear database in the background. This download happens once; afterward the full interlinear — including Hebrew and Greek word data with Strong's numbers and pictographic letter meanings — is stored on your device for offline use.

<Tip>
  Connect to Wi-Fi before opening the interlinear for the first time if you are on a limited mobile data plan.
</Tip>
