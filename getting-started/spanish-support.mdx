Seed Scroll supports Spanish reading and study through **Reina-Valera 1909 Plus (RV1909)** and Spanish app language settings.

## What is available in Spanish

| Area | Spanish support |
|------|-----------------|
| Bible reader | RV1909 Spanish Bible text |
| Strong's study | Spanish-friendly Strong's display for RV1909 where data is available |
| App copy | Spanish navigation and interface copy where localized |
| Bible book names | Spanish book names in supported reader and navigation flows |
| AI studies | Spanish output when Spanish mode or RV1909 context is active |
| AI reading plans | Spanish output when Spanish mode is active |
| Historical context | Spanish context requests when Spanish app language is active |

## Read RV1909

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Bible Reader">
    Go to the **Bible** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the translation picker">
    Tap the current translation label in the reader navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select RV1909">
    Choose **Reina-Valera 1909 Plus**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch app language if prompted">
    Seed Scroll may ask whether you also want to switch the app interface to Spanish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Change app language manually

Go to **Profile → Reader Settings → App language** and choose **Español**.

Spanish mode affects localized app copy and sends supported AI study requests in Spanish.

## Translation and language are separate

Your Bible translation and app language are related, but separate settings.

- You can read RV1909 while keeping the app interface in English.
- You can use Spanish app copy while switching back to an English Bible translation.
- Seed Scroll prompts when your selected Bible translation and app language appear mismatched.

## Current limitations

Spanish support is active, but not every feature has the same depth of localization yet.

- Some community content depends on the language used by the author.
- Some source-backed historical context may not be prepared for every passage.
- Some lexical data depends on the underlying Strong's and translation datasets.
