---
title: "Guest Mode"
description: "Use Seed Scroll without an account — read the full Bible, explore Strong's concordance, and take local study notes."
---

You do not need to create an account to start using Seed Scroll. Guest mode gives you access to the core Bible study features immediately, with study notes saved locally on your device.

## Enter guest mode

You can enter guest mode from two places:

- **Onboarding screen** — tap **Continue as Guest** after swiping through the feature slides.
- **Auth screen** — tap **Continue without account** at the bottom of the sign-in screen.

A confirmation prompt reminds you that notes saved in guest mode are stored on this device only. Tap **Continue anyway** to proceed.

## What you can do in guest mode

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Full Bible reading" icon="book-open">
    Read all 66 books of the KJV+ Bible offline. Your reading position is remembered between sessions.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Strong's concordance" icon="languages">
    Tap any word in a verse to see its Strong's number, Hebrew or Greek definition, transliteration, and pronunciation.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Interlinear view" icon="align-left">
    Browse the full interlinear text word by word, with the original Hebrew or Greek alongside the English.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Local study notes" icon="notebook-pen">
    Create and edit study notes. Notes are saved to your device and persist between sessions.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Study notes created in guest mode are stored only on this device. If you uninstall the app or switch to a different device, those notes are permanently lost unless you create an account and sign in before switching.
</Warning>

## Upgrade from guest to a full account

You can create an account at any time without losing your place in the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Profile tab">
    Tap the **Profile** icon in the bottom navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap 'Create Account' or 'Sign In'">
    The profile screen prompts you to sign up or sign in when you are in guest mode.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete sign-up">
    Choose email, Google, or Apple sign-in and follow the prompts. See [Create an Account](/getting-started/account-setup) for the full walkthrough.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your notes migrate automatically">
    Any study notes you created as a guest are preserved on the device. After signing in, they remain accessible locally. Notes you create going forward are synced to your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Create an account early to make sure your notes are backed up. You can sign up in under a minute with Google or Apple — no email verification required for OAuth sign-in.
</Tip>
