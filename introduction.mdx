## How Seed Scroll works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download and open">
    Install the app from the App Store or Google Play, or open the web version in your browser. No account required to start reading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a free account">
    Sign up with email, Google, or Apple to sync your notes across devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read and study scripture">
    Open any book and chapter. Tap any word to see its Strong's definition and original language. Use AI search to find verses by meaning, not just keywords.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your library">
    Write study notes, generate AI-powered studies from text or audio, create reading plans, and share your work with the community.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

- **Offline-first reader** — read supported Bible translations locally after download or install
- **Spanish support** — RV1909, Spanish app copy, Spanish book names, and Spanish AI output where supported
- **Supported translations** — KJV+, WEB, RV1909, and BSB with clear Strong's and audio availability
- **Strong's Concordance** — Hebrew and Greek definitions, transliteration, pronunciation, and verse usage
- **Verse study panel** — capture quick reflections, bookmark verses, and load historical context from the reader
- **Interlinear view** — original language text alongside the selected study translation
- **Translation-aware search** — search supported translations by keyword, Strong's entries, or AI-assisted meaning
- **AI study generation** — paste text, upload documents or images, import audio, or record audio to generate a full study note
- **Reading plans** — create plans manually, import PDFs, Word documents, CSV templates, or generate multi-day plans with AI
- **Bookmarks and Saved** — bookmark verses, studies, and public notes with colored labels and collections
- **Playlists and collections** — organize Bible passages, audio playlists, personal notes, and saved public notes
- **Social feed** — follow other Bible students, discover public notes and plans, and publish your own
- **Audio transcription** — record sermons or spoken notes and transcribe them on-device

<Note>
  Most reading features are free with a guest or free account. [Premium features](/account/premium) — including AI search, AI study generation, and the social feed — require a subscription.
</Note>
