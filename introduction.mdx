## How Seed Scroll works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download and open">
    Install the app from the App Store or Google Play, or open the web version in your browser. No account required to start reading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a free account">
    Sign up with email, Google, or Apple to sync your notes across devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read and study scripture">
    Open any book and chapter. Tap any word to see its Strong's definition and original language. Use AI search to find verses by meaning, not just keywords.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Build your library">
    Write study notes, generate AI-powered studies from text or audio, create reading plans, and share your work with the community.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Key features

- **Offline-first reader** — all 31,102 KJV+ verses stored locally; no network needed to read
- **Strong's Concordance** — Hebrew and Greek definitions, transliteration, and pronunciation for every word
- **Interlinear view** — original language text alongside English, word by word
- **AI study generation** — paste text, upload a file, or record audio to generate a full study note
- **Reading plans** — AI-generated multi-day schedules on any topic
- **Social feed** — follow other Bible students, discover public notes, and publish your own
- **Audio transcription** — record sermons or spoken notes and transcribe them on-device

<Note>
  Most reading features are free with a guest or free account. [Premium features](/account/premium) — including AI search, AI study generation, and the social feed — require a subscription.
</Note>
