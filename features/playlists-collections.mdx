Playlists and collections help you organize your study content into themed groups. For saved bookmarks, see [Bookmarks and Saved](/features/bookmarks).

## Playlists

A playlist is an ordered list of Bible passages you can play through sequentially — useful for working through a topic or following a curated reading sequence.

### Create a playlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Playlists">
    From the **Studies** tab, tap the playlist icon or go to **My Playlists**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap + New Playlist">
    Give your playlist a title and optional description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add passages">
    Use the passage picker to add Bible references. Reorder them by dragging the handle on each row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and play">
    Tap **Save**. Open the playlist and tap **Play** to move through passages in order using the audio player.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Make a playlist public

Open the playlist and turn on **Public**. Anyone can then see and play it, and it can reach the feed of people who follow you.

Turning **Public** off makes the playlist private again.

<Note>
  People follow **you**, not an individual playlist. Following a creator is how someone sees the playlists they make public — there is no way to follow a single playlist on its own. See [Following](/features/following).
</Note>

### Share a playlist by link

Sharing a link is a separate action from making a playlist public, and you do not have to choose between them.

Open the playlist and tap the share action. On iOS and Android this opens your device share sheet; on web the link is copied to your clipboard.

A **private** playlist can be shared this way too. That creates a link which works for whoever you send it to, without making the playlist public or putting it in anyone's feed. Sharing a private playlist requires an account.

## Collections

A collection is a curated group of saved content, including study notes, public notes, bookmarked verses, and Saved Verse Images.

### Create a collection

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Collections">
    From the **Studies** tab, tap **Collections** (bookmark icon).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap + New Collection">
    Enter a title for your collection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add notes">
    Tap **Add notes** to search your library and add notes to the collection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Add a public note to a collection

When reading a public note in the feed, tap the **Save** or **Add to collection** option to file it in one of your collections.

### Manage Saved Verse Images

Open **Studies → Saved → Verse images** to view scripture art you created from the verse study panel. From there, you can edit, share, or delete each image.

See [Saved Verse Images](/features/saved-verse-images) for the full workflow.
