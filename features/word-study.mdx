title: "Word Study"
description: "Dive deep into any Hebrew or Greek word with AI-powered analysis and pictographic letter meanings."
---

Word Study lets you explore the full depth of a biblical word in its original language. Go beyond a basic definition — see the root, semantic range, related concepts, and for Hebrew, pictographic meanings for each letter.

<Note>
  Full word studies with AI-generated insights and Hebrew letter analysis are premium features. Basic Strong's definitions are free for all users.
</Note>

## Open a word study

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap a Strong's badge">
    In the Bible reader, tap any word's Strong's number badge to open the definition panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Word Study">
    In the definition panel, tap **Word Study** to open the full analysis screen.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore the study">
    The word study includes:
    - Original word, transliteration, and pronunciation
    - Part of speech and root word
    - Full definition and semantic range
    - AI-generated thematic analysis
    - All Bible verses that use this word (searchable)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Hebrew letter analysis

For Hebrew words, Seed Scroll shows the pictographic meaning of each letter in the word. Ancient Hebrew letters each represent a concrete image (e.g., aleph = ox, beth = house), and reading a word through its letters reveals a layered meaning.

Tap any letter in the Hebrew word to see:
- The letter name and pictographic image
- The semantic concept the letter represents
- How it contributes to the word's overall meaning

## Greek semantic synthesis

For Greek words (New Testament), the word study synthesizes how the word is used across different authors and contexts, surfacing patterns in meaning that a single definition might miss.

## Browse all verses using a word

Tap **See all verses** in the word study to open a list of every Bible verse that contains the same Strong's number. This is useful for understanding how a word is used in different contexts across the full canon.
