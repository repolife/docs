---

Follow other Seed Scroll users to see their public study notes and reading plans in your feed. Build a community of students whose insights you find valuable.

## Follow someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find their profile">
    Tap a user's name or avatar anywhere in the app (feed, note detail, discover screen) to open their public profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Follow">
    Tap the **Follow** button on their profile. Their future public notes and plans will appear in your feed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Discover new people to follow

Open the **Discover** screen (tap the compass icon in the Feed or Studies tab) to browse and search for users. The screen shows suggested accounts and lets you search by name.

## Manage who you follow

Go to your **Profile** and tap **Following** to see everyone you follow. Tap **Unfollow** next to any name to stop seeing their content in your feed.

## Your followers

Tap **Followers** on your profile to see who follows you. You'll also receive a notification when someone new follows you.

## Creator Dashboard

If you publish notes and plans regularly, tap **Creator Dashboard** on your profile to see stats on your public content — views, likes, and follower growth.
