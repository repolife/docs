Follow other Seed Scroll users to see their public studies, reading plans, quick reflections, and audio playlists in your feed. Build a community of students whose insights you find valuable.

You follow a **person**, not an individual study, plan, or playlist. Following someone is what brings everything they make public into your feed.

## Follow someone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Find their profile">
    Tap a user's name or avatar anywhere in the app (feed, note detail, discover screen) to open their public profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Follow">
    Tap the **Follow** button on their profile. What they make public from then on appears in your feed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Discover new people to follow

Open **Discover** to browse beyond the people you already follow. It is organised into sections — featured creators, trending topics, new studies, and new and trending reading plans — and each section has a **See all**. You can also browse by category: studies, plans, creators, or `#hashtags`.

## Manage who you follow

Go to your **Profile** and tap **Following** to see everyone you follow. Tap **Unfollow** next to any name to stop seeing their content in your feed.

## Your followers

Tap **Followers** on your profile to see who follows you. You'll also receive a notification when someone new follows you.

## Creator Dashboard

Open your own profile and tap the chart icon in the header to see how your public work is doing:

- Your follower count
- Total likes across your studies
- Total enrollments across your reading plans
- Each study, ordered by likes
- Each reading plan, ordered by enrollments

The dashboard is on your own profile only — you cannot see anyone else's.

## Blocking

Blocking removes any follow between two people, in both directions, and unblocking does not restore it. See [Reporting & Blocking](/features/reporting-and-blocking).
