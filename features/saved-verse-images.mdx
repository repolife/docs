Saved Verse Images let you turn the current Bible verse into shareable scripture art. Choose a curated background, pick a font, save the image, or share it from your device.

## Create a verse image

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a verse">
    In the **Bible Reader**, long-press the verse you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Images">
    In the verse study panel header, tap the **Images** action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    Swipe through the background carousel and select an admin-curated template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a font">
    Open the font picker and choose a style such as **Classic**, **Elegant**, **Warm**, or **Modern**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or share">
    Tap **Save** to keep the image in Seed Scroll, or use the share action to open your device share sheet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What appears on the image

A Saved Verse Image includes:

- the verse text exactly as it appears in the selected translation
- the Bible reference with the translation abbreviation, such as `John 3:16 (WEB)`
- subtle Seed Scroll branding near the lower-right corner
- the selected template, text alignment, text size, and font

<Note>
  Seed Scroll does not add quotation marks or punctuation to the verse text. The image uses the verse text from the selected Bible translation.
</Note>

## Find saved verse images

Open **Studies → Saved → Verse images** to view the images you saved.

From the **Verse images** collection, you can:

- preview each saved image
- share an image again
- edit the image design
- delete an image everywhere it appears in your saved images

## Template backgrounds

Verse image templates are curated by Seed Scroll. You choose from the available backgrounds instead of uploading your own image or generating one with AI.

This keeps the image library consistent, safe, and ready for sharing.

<Note>
  Saved Verse Images are local-first. If you reinstall the app or move to a new device before sync support is available, saved images may not carry over automatically.
</Note>
