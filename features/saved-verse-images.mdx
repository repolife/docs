Saved Verse Images let you turn a Bible verse or same-chapter passage into shareable scripture art. Choose a curated background, pick a font, save the image, or share it from your device.

## Create a verse image

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a verse">
    In the **Bible Reader**, long-press the verse you want to use.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Images">
    In the verse study panel header, tap the **Images** action.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a template">
    Swipe through the background carousel and select an admin-curated template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a passage">
    Keep the current verse, or expand it into a same-chapter passage when you want to share more context.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a font">
    Open the font picker and choose a style such as **Classic**, **Elegant**, **Warm**, **Script**, or **Modern**. On mobile, the font picker opens as a bottom sheet for easier one-handed selection.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save or share">
    Tap **Save** to keep the image in Seed Scroll, or use the share action to open your device share sheet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What appears on the image

A Saved Verse Image includes:

- the verse or passage text exactly as it appears in the selected translation
- the Bible reference with the translation abbreviation, such as `John 3:16 (WEB)` or `John 1:1-5 (WEB)`
- subtle Seed Scroll branding near the lower-right corner
- the selected template, text alignment, text size, and font

<Note>
  Seed Scroll does not add quotation marks or punctuation to the verse text. The image uses the verse text from the selected Bible translation.
</Note>

## Passage fit

Passage images use compact, fit-aware layouts for longer selections. If a passage is too long to stay readable on the selected template and text size, Seed Scroll asks you to shorten the passage or choose a more compact style before saving or sharing.

Recent layout fixes improve text fitting and font picker behavior, so long passages are less likely to clip and selected font styles update the preview before you save or share.

## Three ways to finish an image

The editor offers three actions, and each leaves behind something different.

| Action | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| **Save** | The image is kept in Seed Scroll. Nothing leaves your device |
| **Save image** | Hands the picture file to your device — keep it in your photos, or send it as an attachment |
| **Share link** | Creates a web address anyone can open to see the image |

**Save** now confirms where the image went — *Verse image saved to Studies → Verse images* — with a **View** action that takes you straight there. You are returned to what you were reading, so the confirmation is how you reach the image without hunting for it.

The two outward actions leave the editor open. Backing out of your device's share sheet returns you to your work rather than discarding it.

## Share an image by link

**Share link** publishes the image to a web address. Anyone you send it to can open it and see the image, whether or not they use Seed Scroll. The link is copied for you as it is created.

Creating a link needs an account, since the published image belongs to yours.

<Note>
  A link makes the image readable by anyone who has it. Treat it the way you would treat any address you send out — there is no password on it.
</Note>

## Take a link down

Open **Studies → Saved → Verse images**, choose the image, and tap **Unshare**.

The link stops working for everyone you sent it to. The image stays in your saved images — only the public copy goes away.

The same screen offers **Copy link** while an image is shared, so you can send it again without creating a second one.

## Find saved verse images

Open **Studies → Saved → Verse images** to view the images you saved.

From the **Verse images** collection, you can:

- preview each saved image
- share an image again, as a file or as a link
- copy a link you already created, or take it down
- edit the image design
- delete an image everywhere it appears in your saved images

## Template backgrounds

Verse image templates are curated by Seed Scroll. You choose from the available backgrounds instead of uploading your own image or generating one with AI.

The template library includes parchment, soft light, ocean, desert path, linen, sunrise, and warm gradient backgrounds. Each template is chosen to keep scripture readable while giving the image a distinct mood.

Seed Scroll caches template backgrounds locally after they load, so reopening the editor is faster and uses less network data.

This keeps the image library consistent, safe, and ready for sharing.

<Note>
  Saved Verse Images are local-first. If you reinstall the app or move to a new device before sync support is available, saved images may not carry over automatically.
</Note>
