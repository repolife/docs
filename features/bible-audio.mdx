Bible audio lets you listen to scripture from the reader, reading plans, and saved playlists.

<Note>
  Bible audio is available in the mobile app. Browser support is limited because audio playback depends on native mobile audio APIs.
</Note>

## Translation support

| Translation | Audio support |
|-------------|---------------|
| KJV+ | Yes |
| WEB | Yes |
| RV1909 | Yes |
| BSB | Not yet |

If your active translation does not support audio yet, Seed Scroll can prompt you to switch to an audio-capable translation.

## Play a chapter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open a chapter">
    Go to the **Bible Reader** and open the chapter you want to hear.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap play">
    Tap the audio play button in the reader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow along">
    As audio plays, Seed Scroll highlights the active verse and can scroll with playback.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the player">
    Tap the mini-player to open the full audio sheet with playback controls.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Listen to reading plans

Reading plans can be played when their assigned passages use an audio-supported translation.

From a reading plan day, start playback to listen through the assigned passages. Seed Scroll keeps the queue connected to the plan day so you can read and listen together.

## Use audio playlists

Audio playlists are saved queues of Bible passages. Use them for topical listening, memorization, commuting, or group reading.

You can:

- create a playlist from the **Studies** area
- add Bible passages to a playlist
- play through passages in order
- save playlists for later
- publish playlists to your profile when sharing is available

## When audio is unavailable

Audio may be unavailable when:

- the selected translation does not support audio yet
- a specific passage has not been recorded
- you are using the web app
- premium access is required for the audio flow you are trying to use

Seed Scroll shows a message instead of silently failing.
