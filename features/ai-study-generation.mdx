The AI study generator creates a fully structured study note from source material you provide. Paste a sermon transcript, upload documents, snap a photo of notes, import audio, or record audio — the AI extracts the key themes, cross-references scripture, and produces a study you can save and edit.

<Note>
  AI study generation is a premium feature. [Upgrade to premium](/account/premium) to access it.
</Note>

## Supported input types

| Input | Formats |
|-------|---------|
| Pasted text | Any plain text or markdown |
| Document | PDF, Word (.docx), plain text (.txt), markdown |
| Image | JPEG, PNG — the AI reads text from the image |
| Audio | MP3, MP4, WAV, AAC, M4A, FLAC — transcribed then studied |
| Live recording | Record directly in the app (up to 2 hours) |

<Note>
  Generated studies follow your active study preferences and reader translation where supported. For example, RV1909 study output is generated in Spanish. AI features also include content-safety checks before generated or synced study content is published.
</Note>

## Generate a study

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the generate screen">
    From the **Studies** tab, tap the **+** button and choose **AI-generated**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your input">
    Select how you want to provide source material:
    - **Paste text** — type or paste content directly
    - **Import file** — pick a document from your device
    - **Photo** — take or choose a photo containing text
    - **Record audio** — tap the microphone to record; transcription runs on-device
    - **Import audio** — pick an existing audio file from your device
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a title and hashtags (optional)">
    Give your study a title and add topic hashtags. These can also be edited after generation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Generate">
    The AI processes your input and builds the study note. Generation typically takes 15–60 seconds depending on content length.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review and save">
    The generated study opens in the editor. Review the content, make any edits, then tap **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Generate from Bible references

You can also start AI study generation from Bible references you are already using:

- Tap the **+ icon** beside a single search result, then choose **AI-generated**.
- Select multiple search results, tap **Study**, then choose **AI-generated**.
- From **Cross-References**, choose one or more related verses and tap **Study**.
- From **Studies → Saved → Bible verses**, start a study from a saved verse bookmark.
- At the end of a Bible chapter, choose the chapter study action or use the Chapter Guide to start a deeper AI study.

Seed Scroll creates a starter prompt from the selected reference or references, then opens the AI generation screen for review before you generate the study.

## Study preferences

In **Reader Settings**, choose a study style and preferences such as simple, guided, or deeper study. These preferences influence AI-generated studies, titles, hashtags, and reading plans.

You can also expand or improve existing AI-generated notes later by adding more source material or asking for a focused refinement.

## Tips for better results

<Tip>
  Longer, richer input produces better studies. A full sermon transcript or detailed passage commentary gives the AI more to work with than a few sentences.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  For audio recordings, a quiet environment improves transcription accuracy. The transcription runs entirely on-device — your audio is never uploaded.
</Tip>

## File size limits

Individual files must be under 20 MB. Audio recordings are capped at 2 hours. If your file exceeds these limits, split it into smaller sections before importing.
