Cross-references are verse-to-verse connections that link related passages across the Bible — verses that share a theme, quote the same source, or shed light on each other. Seed Scroll stores all cross-reference data locally in a 22 MB database, so the feature works without a network connection.

## How to open cross-references for a verse

<Steps>
  <Step title="Long-press a verse">
    Press and hold any verse in the reader to open the verse study panel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the Cross-References tab">
    In the panel, tap the **Cross-References** tab (the network icon). The panel shows the number of cross-references found for that verse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a cross-reference to open it">
    Tap any result to navigate directly to that verse in the reader.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Verses with cross-references show a verse indicator dot beside the verse number. You can spot them at a glance while reading.
</Tip>

## Filtering results by book

When a verse has many cross-references, you can filter the list to a single Bible book:

1. Tap the **book dropdown** at the top of the cross-references screen.
2. Select a book name. The count beside each book shows how many cross-references come from that book.
3. Tap **All books** to clear the filter and return to the full list.

## Navigating to a referenced verse

Tap any cross-reference result to open that verse in the Bible Reader. The reader navigates to the exact book, chapter, and verse. Use the back button to return to your previous location.

## How cross-references are ranked

Results are ordered by **vote count** — a measure of how strongly that connection is supported across multiple reference works. Higher-voted connections appear first.

<Note>
  Cross-reference data comes from a curated database of verse connections compiled from classical concordances and reference works. The data is bundled with the app and does not change unless you update Seed Scroll.
</Note>
