The Feed is what the people you follow have made public. It is a place to learn from others, share what you are studying, and stay connected with fellow Bible students.

<Note>
  The feed requires a free account. [Sign in or create an account](/getting-started/account-setup) to access it.
</Note>

## Feed and Discover are different

**Feed** shows only the people you already follow. If you follow no one, it is empty.

**Discover** is where you find people you do not follow yet. It is a separate screen, reached from the tab at the top of the feed or from the compass icon.

Following someone is what moves their work from Discover into your feed.

## Browse the feed

Tap **Feed** in the bottom navigation bar. It carries four kinds of public content from the people you follow:

- **Studies** — full study notes
- **Reading plans**
- **Quick reflections** — short thoughts on a single verse
- **Audio playlists**

The filters across the top narrow this: **All** shows everything, **Studies** shows study notes together with quick reflections, and **Reading Plans** shows plans on their own.

Tap any card to open it in full.

<Note>
  There are no comments and no general posts. Everything in the feed is one of the four kinds above, made public by the person who created it.
</Note>

## Like a study

Tap the heart icon on a study to like it. The author is notified.

## Share or download a public study

Tap the share icon on any public note to send it via your device's share sheet. You can share a link via message, email, or any other app.

Open a public study detail page and tap the PDF action to download or share a formatted PDF copy of the study. The PDF includes the study title, author, scripture text, note content, word-study blocks, hashtags, and safe public video links when available.

## Copy a study to your library

Tap **Copy to My Notes** on a public study to create your own editable copy in your Studies library.

The copy is yours: private, and free to edit and annotate. **The original creator's study is not affected**, and copying does not publish anything of yours.

## Discover new people

Tap **Discover** to find Bible students you do not follow yet. Search by name, or browse what is featured there.

## If something does not belong

You can report a study, reading plan, or audio playlist, and you can block the account that made it. See [Reporting & Blocking](/features/reporting-and-blocking).
