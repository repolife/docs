The Feed is a stream of public study notes and reading plans shared by the people you follow and the broader Seed Scroll community. It's a place to learn from others, share what you're studying, and stay connected with fellow Bible students.

<Note>
  The social feed requires a free account. [Sign in or create an account](/getting-started/account-setup) to access it.
</Note>

## Browse the feed

Tap **Feed** in the bottom navigation bar. The feed shows:

- Study notes published by people you follow
- Reading plans shared publicly
- Activity from the community (likes, new followers)

Scroll through the feed and tap any note or plan to read the full content.

## Like and react

Tap the heart icon on any note to like it. The author receives a notification. You can see all your liked notes from your profile.

## Share a note from the feed

Tap the share icon on any public note to send it via your device's share sheet — you can share a link via message, email, or any other app.

## Copy a note to your library

Tap **Copy to my notes** on any public note to save a private copy to your own Studies library. You can then edit and annotate it freely.

## Discover new people

Tap **Discover** (accessible from the Feed or Studies tab) to find other Bible students to follow. You can search by name or browse featured creators.
