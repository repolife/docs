Seed Scroll sends notifications for social activity, finished AI generations, and reading reminders. Most categories can be turned on or off inside the app.

## Notification types

| Notification | When it's sent |
|---|---|
| New follower | Someone follows you |
| Study interaction | Someone likes, copies, or saves one of your study notes |
| Reading plan activity | Someone likes, enrolls in, or completes one of your reading plans |
| New work from someone you follow | A creator you follow publishes a study note, reading plan, or audio playlist |
| AI generation finished | Your AI study or reading plan is ready — or could not be finished |
| Reading reminder | Your scheduled daily reading time |

## AI generation alerts

AI studies and reading plans are generated on Seed Scroll's servers, so you do not have to sit and watch the progress screen. Leave the app, lock your phone, or read something else — you get a notification when the work is finished.

- **Finished** — your study or plan is ready. Open the app and Seed Scroll takes you straight to it.
- **Could not be finished** — generation failed. Open the app to see the reason and try again.

If the app is already open when generation finishes, your phone gives a short buzz instead.

<Note>
  AI generation alerts are not covered by the toggles below. They tell you about work you started yourself, so they are always delivered. Turning off notifications for Seed Scroll in your device settings silences them along with everything else.
</Note>

## Choose which alerts you get

Device alerts are controlled per category in Seed Scroll:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reader Settings">
    Tap **Profile** → **Settings** → **Reader Settings**, or tap the settings icon in the Bible reader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find Push Notifications">
    Scroll to the **Push Notifications** section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Toggle the categories you want">
    - **New followers**
    - **Study interactions** — likes, copies, and saves
    - **Reading plan activity** — likes, enrollments, and completions
    - **New work from people you follow** — studies, reading plans, and audio playlists
  </Step>
</Steps>

Turning a category off stops the device alert. The activity still appears in your in-app notifications inbox.

## Reading reminders

Set a daily reminder to keep your reading habit on track.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Reader Settings">
    Tap **Profile** → **Settings** → **Reader Settings**, or tap the settings icon in the Bible reader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Reading Reminder">
    Toggle on **Daily Reading Reminder** and choose a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm permissions">
    If prompted, allow Seed Scroll to send notifications. You can change this anytime in your device's notification settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View your notifications

Tap the **Feed** tab and then tap the bell icon in the top-right corner to open your notifications inbox. Unread notifications show a badge count on the Feed tab icon.

## Manage notification permissions

The toggles above control what Seed Scroll sends. To silence the app entirely, go to your device's **Settings → Notifications → Seed Scroll** and turn notifications off there.
