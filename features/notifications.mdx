title: "Notifications"
description: "Stay informed about new followers, likes, comments, and your daily reading reminders."
---

Seed Scroll sends notifications for social activity and reading reminders. You can control what you receive in your device settings.

## Notification types

| Notification | When it's sent |
|---|---|
| New follower | Someone follows you |
| Like | Someone likes your published note or plan |
| Reading reminder | Your scheduled daily reading time |

## Reading reminders

Set a daily reminder to keep your reading habit on track.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Reader Settings">
    Tap **Profile** → **Settings** → **Reader Settings**, or tap the settings icon in the Bible reader.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable Reading Reminder">
    Toggle on **Daily Reading Reminder** and choose a time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm permissions">
    If prompted, allow Seed Scroll to send notifications. You can change this anytime in your device's notification settings.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## View your notifications

Tap the **Feed** tab and then tap the bell icon in the top-right corner to open your notifications inbox. Unread notifications show a badge count on the Feed tab icon.

## Manage notification permissions

To change which notifications you receive, go to your device's **Settings → Notifications → Seed Scroll** and adjust the permissions there.
