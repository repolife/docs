Seed Scroll lets you read from multiple supported Bible translations. The active translation controls the Bible text shown in the reader, search context, study creation context, and passage navigation.

## Supported translations

| Translation | Label | Language | Strong's study | Audio |
|-------------|-------|----------|----------------|-------|
| King James Version Plus | KJV+ | English | Native word-level Strong's | Yes |
| World English Bible | WEB | English | KJV-backed verse-level study | Yes |
| Reina-Valera 1909 Plus | RV1909 | Spanish | Native word-level Strong's | Yes |
| Berean Standard Bible | BSB | English | Native word-level Strong's | Not yet |

<Note>
  Audio availability depends on the selected translation. If a translation does not currently support audio, Seed Scroll will let you switch to an audio-capable translation instead of failing silently.
</Note>

## Change translations

Open the **Bible Reader** and tap the translation label in the navigation bar. Choose the translation you want to read.

The first time you select a translation that is not already downloaded, Seed Scroll downloads the local Bible database for offline reading.

## Strong's support by translation

Seed Scroll is honest about how Strong's study works across translations:

- **KJV+**, **RV1909**, and **BSB** include native Strong's tagging.
- **WEB** is a public-domain reading translation. It does not include native Strong's tagging, so Seed Scroll uses the corresponding KJV+ verse as the lexical study backbone.

This means WEB readers can still open Strong's study tools, but the app does not claim exact word-level WEB-to-Greek/Hebrew precision when the underlying data does not support it.

## Spanish reading

When you select **RV1909**, Seed Scroll can also prompt you to switch the app language to Spanish. This keeps Bible text, book names, and app copy aligned for Spanish study.

See [Spanish Support](/getting-started/spanish-support) for the full language workflow.

## Offline reading

Supported Bible translations are downloaded or bundled for offline reading. After a translation database is available on your device, you can read without an internet connection.

AI features, social content, sync, and first-time downloads still require network access.
