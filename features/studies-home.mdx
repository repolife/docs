The **Studies** tab is your hub for personal study work. It brings together study notes, quick reflections, reading plans, audio playlists, saved items, and activity from people you follow.

## Main sections

Use the top tabs to switch between study areas:

| Tab | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Notes** | Your study notes, quick reflections, topics, and recent notes |
| **Following** | Public notes and quick reflections from people you follow |
| **Plans** | Reading plans and plan progress |
| **Saved** | Saved collections, including saved verse images |
| **Audio** | Playlists and audio collections |

## Create from the Add menu

On the **Notes** and **Plans** tabs, tap the floating **Add** button to open creation actions.

Depending on the tab and your account settings, actions can include:

- creating a manual study note
- generating a study with AI
- recording audio for transcription
- creating a reading plan
- generating a reading plan with AI

The expanded action menu uses a soft glass-style backing so the actions stay readable when study cards or long text scroll underneath them.

<Note>
  Disabled AI actions remain visible when AI features are unavailable, so you can see what is possible without losing access to manual creation tools.
</Note>

## Saved collections

The **Saved** tab collects saved content such as bookmarks and saved verse images. Open **Verse images** to review scripture art you created from the verse study panel.

## Following activity

The **Following** tab shows public study notes and quick reflections from people you follow. Public quick reflections appear separately from full study notes so shorter verse responses are easier to scan.
