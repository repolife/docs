Reading plans give you a structured schedule for working through scripture. You can create your own plan, import an existing schedule, generate one with AI, or follow plans shared by other users.

## Create a reading plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reading Plans">
    From the **Studies** tab, tap **Reading Plans** (calendar icon).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap + New Plan">
    Choose **Create manually** to build a plan yourself, **Generate with AI** to have the app create one for you, or import a spreadsheet template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the plan details">
    Enter a title, optional description, and the number of days. For AI generation, also describe the topic or theme (e.g., "30 days through the Psalms" or "Study on forgiveness").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add readings (manual)">
    For each day, add one or more Bible passages. Use the passage picker to select a book, chapter, and verse range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and start">
    Tap **Save**. Your plan appears in the Reading Plans list. Tap **Start** to begin Day 1.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Import a reading plan

Seed Scroll can turn structured reading schedules into editable plans.

Supported import paths include:

| Import type | Best for |
|-------------|----------|
| Filled CSV template | Precise day-by-day schedules |
| Pasted text or converted table text | Existing church, ministry, or fellowship plans |
| Documents | PDFs, Word documents, text files, and markdown files |

<Tip>
  For complex PDFs, convert the PDF to text, markdown, or CSV first when possible. Table-based text is easier to parse than raw PDF layout.
</Tip>

After import, Seed Scroll opens the plan editor so you can review each day before saving or publishing.

## AI-generated plans

<Note>
  AI reading plan generation is a premium feature. [Upgrade to premium](/account/premium) to use it.
</Note>

Describe any topic and the AI builds a multi-day plan with curated passages and a brief note for each day. You can edit the generated plan before saving it.

You can also provide source material such as sermon notes, devotional outlines, or a structured reading schedule. The app will try to extract Bible references and build day-by-day entries.

## Follow a plan

Work through your reading plan day by day:

- Open the plan and tap the current day
- The Bible reader opens directly to the assigned passage
- Complete the whole day or mark individual chapters complete
- Play a day or full plan with the audio player when audio is available
- Add reflections for a reading day
- Your progress is saved so you can pick up where you left off

## Share a plan

You can publish a reading plan to your public profile so others can follow it. Open the plan, tap the share icon, and choose **Publish**. Followers will see it in their feed.
