Reading plans give you a structured schedule for working through scripture. You can create your own plan, import an existing schedule, generate one with AI, or follow plans shared by other users.

## Create a reading plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Reading Plans">
    From the **Studies** tab, tap **Reading Plans** (calendar icon).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap + New Plan">
    Choose **Create manually** to build a plan yourself, **Generate with AI** to have the app create one for you, or import a spreadsheet template.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the plan details">
    Enter a title, optional description, and the number of days. For AI generation, also describe the topic or theme (e.g., "30 days through the Psalms" or "Study on forgiveness").
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add readings (manual)">
    For each day, add one or more Bible passages. Use the passage picker to select a book, chapter, and optional verse range, or use **Add passage range** for one continuous same-book range.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and start">
    Tap **Save**. Your plan appears in the Reading Plans list. Tap **Start** to begin Day 1.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use a passage range

Use **Add passage range** when one reading day should cover a continuous span, such as `Genesis 1:1–3:7`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Add passage range">
    In the day editor, go to **Passages** and tap **Add passage range**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the start">
    Choose the book, start chapter, and start verse.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the end">
    Choose the end chapter and end verse, then tap **Add range to day**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Seed Scroll expands the range into per-chapter readings. For `Genesis 1:1–3:7`, the day includes `Genesis 1:1` through the end of chapter 1, all of `Genesis 2`, and `Genesis 3:1–7`.

<Tip>
  You can mix individual passages and passage ranges on the same day.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Passage ranges are currently same-book only. The ending verse must come after the starting verse.
</Warning>

## Import a reading plan

Seed Scroll can turn structured reading schedules into editable plans. Use this when you already have a church schedule, ministry PDF, spreadsheet, devotional calendar, or personal plan.

Supported import paths include:

| Import type | Best for | Notes |
|-------------|----------|-------|
| Filled CSV template | Precise day-by-day schedules | Most reliable import path |
| PDF | Existing printable reading schedules | Works best when the PDF has readable text or clear tables |
| Word document | Plans drafted in a document editor | Supports `.doc` and `.docx` imports |
| Plain text or markdown | Copied schedules, outlines, and exported notes | Good for simple day-by-day lists |
| Pasted text | Quick conversion from a website, email, or message | Paste the schedule into the prompt field |

### Import with the CSV template

Use the CSV template when you want the most predictable result.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Download the template">
    In the reading plan generator, choose the spreadsheet/template option and download `reading-plan-template.csv`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in plan details">
    Add a plan title, optional description, and one row per passage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use Bible book numbers">
    The template includes common book-number examples, such as Genesis `1`, Psalms `19`, John `43`, Romans `45`, and Revelation `66`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Repeat days for multiple passages">
    Use the same `day_number` on multiple rows when a day includes more than one passage.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Import and review">
    Upload the completed CSV. Seed Scroll opens the plan editor so you can review every day before saving.
  </Step>
</Steps>

CSV columns:

| Column | Required | Description |
|--------|----------|-------------|
| `day_number` | Yes | The reading day number |
| `day_title` | No | Optional title for the day |
| `book` | Yes | Bible book number |
| `chapter` | Yes | Chapter number |
| `verse_start` | No | Starting verse for a partial-chapter reading |
| `verse_end` | No | Ending verse for a partial-chapter reading |
| `note` | No | Reflection prompt or day note |

### Import from PDF or document

For PDFs and documents, Seed Scroll extracts the reading schedule and converts it into days.

Good source files usually have:

- one reading day per row or paragraph
- visible day numbers or dates
- clear Bible references
- minimal decorative layout
- readable text instead of scanned images

<Tip>
  For complex PDFs, export or copy the table to CSV, text, or markdown first when possible. Table-based text is easier to parse than raw PDF layout.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  Always review imported plans before saving or publishing. PDF layouts, scanned documents, and unusual abbreviations can cause missed or incorrect references.
</Warning>

After import, Seed Scroll opens the plan editor so you can correct titles, passages, verse ranges, and notes before starting the plan.

## AI-generated plans

<Note>
  AI reading plan generation is a premium feature. [Upgrade to premium](/account/premium) to use it.
</Note>

Describe any topic and the AI builds a multi-day plan with curated passages and a brief note for each day. You can edit the generated plan before saving it.

You can also provide source material such as sermon notes, devotional outlines, or a structured reading schedule. The app will try to extract Bible references and build day-by-day entries.

Plans built from an **imported document** run as a background job on Seed Scroll's servers. You can leave the app while one is generating — a notification tells you when your plan is ready, or if it could not be finished, and returning to the generator picks it back up for review. If you are still in the app, your phone gives a short buzz instead.

Plans generated from a **description you type** are produced while you wait, so stay on the screen until the review step appears. See [Notifications](/features/notifications).

## Follow a plan

Work through your reading plan day by day:

- Open the plan and tap the current day
- The Bible reader opens directly to the assigned passage
- Complete the whole day or mark individual chapters complete
- Play a day or full plan with the audio player when audio is available
- Add reflections for a reading day
- Your progress is saved so you can pick up where you left off

## Share a plan

You can publish a reading plan to your public profile so others can follow it. Open the plan, tap the share icon, and choose **Publish**. Followers will see it in their feed.
